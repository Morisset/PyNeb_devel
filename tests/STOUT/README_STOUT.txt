Minimal extract of the Stout database for the PyNeb test suite, truncated to
the first 5 levels.
Used by tests/test_stout.py through pn.config.set_stout_path().

  o_3  (Cloudy c23.01): a single TEMP block.
  ne_3 (Cloudy c25.00): two TEMP blocks with different temperature grids and
                        different level coverage (the first block holds levels
                        1-3 on a 27-point grid, the second levels 1-5 on a
                        10-point one).
